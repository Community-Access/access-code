The optional Piper LibriTTS high model was converted for sherpa-onnx:
https://huggingface.co/csukuangfj/vits-piper-en_US-libritts-high/tree/b436d2684d7de5ded1112f173a742f5d47a40e89
The original model card reports training from scratch on LibriTTS train-clean-360:
https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices/blob/375a0fe641dea077c2a47b4e9a056d6da521eed3/en/en_US/libritts/high/MODEL_CARD
LibriTTS is prepared by Heiga Zen with assistance from Google Speech and
Google Brain team members, and is distributed under CC BY 4.0:
https://www.openslr.org/60/
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
Zen et al., "LibriTTS: A Corpus Derived from LibriSpeech for Text-to-Speech",
arXiv, 2019, https://arxiv.org/abs/1904.02882.

The optional OpenPhonemizer US English ONNX model and dictionary are from
OpenPhonemizer, converted by the Eternia launcher contributors:
https://huggingface.co/nekwo/openphonemizer-en-us-onnx/tree/1e8cb072f1c2aa17286f235a7740b596ac7bea31
