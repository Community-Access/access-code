The optional Misaki US English ONNX phonemizer contains word lists and G2P
logic from misaki, the PeterReid/graphemes_to_phonemes_en_us fallback model,
and the hexgrad/Kokoro-82M vocabulary. Its ONNX conversion and packaging were
made for the Eternia launcher. The phonemizer's model card identifies these
components as Apache-2.0:

https://huggingface.co/nekwo/misaki-en-us-g2p-onnx/tree/4b3a605d2f605318ab4f762ab9855c44106528b7

The optional Kokoro pack includes a copy of the Apache License 2.0.
